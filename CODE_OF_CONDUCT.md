# Community rules

Be useful. Be honest. Treat people with respect.

- Explain corrections with sources. Disagree with a claim without attacking the person.
- No harassment, impersonation, spam, personal information or undisclosed commercial interests.
- No invented checks, misleading prices, malicious links or attempts to take another contributor’s referral reward.
- Do not turn quantity of posts or votes into a substitute for evidence.

The maintainer may reject edits, remove harmful content, protect pages or restrict access. Keep an explanation in the private moderation record. Public histories can be suppressed where they expose private or harmful material. Serious issues and appeals go through the private contact listed in SECURITY.md.
